        About PLUGE

        Named after the darkest bars on the test card

        PLUGE stands for picture line-up generation equipment: the strip of near-black bars that engineers used to set a screen's black level. Each drop takes one thing from broadcast TV and builds a handful of pieces around it.

        Nothing is printed until you order it, so there's no pile of unsold stock. Flick through the channels to see Drop 01 on air.
