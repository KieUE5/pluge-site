        About

        Named after the darkest bars on the test card

        PLUGE stands for picture line-up generation equipment: the strip of near-black bars in the corner of a test card that engineers used to set a screen's black level. PLUGE makes heavyweight streetwear in small runs, and each drop takes one thing from broadcast TV and builds a handful of pieces around it.

        Fleece is 450gsm and brushed on the inside. Tees are 280gsm and preshrunk, so the size you buy is the size you keep.
