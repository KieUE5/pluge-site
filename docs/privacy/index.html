Privacy and cookies

Last updated 12 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information PLUGE collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

PLUGE is a trading name of [your name], a sole trader based in the UK. Our business address is [business address]. We decide how your information is used, which makes us its "controller" under UK data protection law.

For anything about your information: Email pluge.shop@outlook.com.

What we collect and why

WhenWhatWhy, and the legal basis
You place an orderName, delivery and billing address, email, phone number if you give it, what you bought, and payment statusTo take payment, deliver your order, handle returns and keep the records the law requires. Basis: our contract with you, and legal obligation for tax records.
You create an accountName, email, a password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it), your bag, saved and recently viewed pieces, and the orders placed with your emailTo run your account, keep your bag on all your devices and show your orders. Basis: our contract with you.
You sign up for drop alertsEmail address, and when and how you agreedTo email you about new drops. Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email.
You contact us, by email or with the contact formYour name, email, order number if you give it, and your messageTo reply and sort out your question. Basis: our legitimate interest in helping customers.

We don't use analytics, advertising trackers or social media pixels on this website, and we don't sell your information.

Cookies and storage on your device

This website doesn't set cookies. To make the shop work, it keeps a few things in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • your bag, so it's still there when you come back;
  • the pieces you've saved, and the ones you've recently viewed;
  • whether you've paused the site's motion.

If you sign in, it also keeps a sign-in token there so you stay signed in, and your bag, saved and recently viewed pieces are copied to your account. Otherwise none of this is sent to us. You can delete it at any time by signing out or clearing this site's data in your browser settings. When you check out, our checkout provider (Shopify) sets cookies needed to take payment; its cookie notice applies there.

Who we share it with

  • Shopify, which runs our checkout and stores order details for us.
  • Supabase, which hosts customer accounts and stores the account information above for us.
  • Payment providers (such as Shopify Payments and PayPal), which handle your payment and check it for fraud.
  • Printful, which makes your order and posts it to you, so it needs your name, delivery address and what you bought. Its delivery partners need your name and address too.
  • Our email providers, which carry the emails we send and receive, including drop alerts if you sign up.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website and keeps short-term server logs, including IP addresses, for security.
  • Our accountants and advisers, and HMRC or the police when the law requires it.

Some of these companies store information outside the UK. When they do, they use safeguards recognised by UK law, such as adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.

How long we keep it

  • Order records: six years after the end of the financial year of the order, as tax law requires.
  • Accounts: until you delete yours, which you can do yourself from the account panel. Order records stay for six years, as above.
  • Drop alert sign-ups: until you unsubscribe.
  • Messages: up to two years after we last hear from you.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or send it to you or someone else in a portable form. You can object to us using it, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Email us and we'll respond within one month. You can also delete your account yourself at any time.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.
