Returns and refunds

Last updated 12 October 2026

Changed your mind? You have two ways to send pieces back: our free 30-day returns, and your legal right to cancel within 14 days. Use whichever suits you.

Free returns within 30 days

  • Send back unworn pieces with their tags attached within 30 days of delivery.
  • Email [email address] with your order number and we'll send you a free returns label.
  • We refund to your original payment method within 14 days of receiving the pieces.

Your legal right to cancel

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you can cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of the day you receive it (or the last parcel, if it came in several).

  1. Tell us within the 14 days. Email [email address] saying you want to cancel, or fill in the form below and email or post it to us. You don't have to give a reason.
  2. Send the pieces back within 14 days of telling us, to [returns address]. We'll send you a free returns label.
  3. We refund you within 14 days of receiving the pieces, or of you showing us proof you've sent them, whichever is sooner. The refund includes the cost of standard delivery. If you chose Royal Mail Tracked 24, we refund £4.95, the cost of our standard service.

You can try pieces on as you would in a shop. If a piece comes back worn, washed or damaged, we may reduce the refund by the amount its value has gone down.

Faulty pieces

Faulty pieces are covered by the Consumer Rights Act 2015, whatever the time limits above. See Terms of sale for what that means, then email us with a photo of the fault.
