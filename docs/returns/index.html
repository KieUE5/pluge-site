Returns and refunds

Last updated 12 October 2026

Every piece is made to order for you, but you can still change your mind. You have 14 days from delivery to cancel, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.

How to return something

  1. Tell us within 14 days of the day your order arrives (or the last parcel, if it came in several). Email pluge.shop@outlook.com saying you want to cancel, or fill in the form below and email or post it to us. You don't have to give a reason.
  2. Send the pieces back within 14 days of telling us, to [returns address]. Use a tracked service and keep your proof of postage. Return postage is yours to pay, unless the piece is faulty.
  3. We refund you within 14 days of receiving the pieces, or of you showing us proof you've sent them, whichever is sooner. The refund goes back to your original payment method and includes the delivery charge you paid.

You can try pieces on as you would in a shop. If a piece comes back worn, washed or damaged, we may reduce the refund by the amount its value has gone down.

Faulty pieces

If a piece arrives faulty, damaged or misprinted, email us a photo within 30 days and we'll replace it or refund you, and you won't pay to send it back. Faulty pieces are covered by the Consumer Rights Act 2015 whatever the time limits above; see Terms of sale.
