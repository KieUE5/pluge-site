Orders and payment

How does made to order work?

Nothing is printed until you order it. Our print partner makes your piece, usually within 2–5 working days, then posts it straight to you. It means we don't throw away unsold stock, and it's why delivery takes a little longer than a warehouse would.

How do I pay?

You pay on our secure checkout, run by Shopify, by card or with the wallet options it shows you, such as Apple Pay or Google Pay. We never see your full card details.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Email pluge.shop@outlook.com as soon as you can with your order number. We can change or cancel it until it goes into production, which often starts within a few hours. After that, you can still return it within 14 days of delivery.

Do I need an account to order?

No. An account keeps your bag, saved pieces and sizes on all your devices and shows your orders, but you can check out without one.

Where do I enter a discount code?

In your bag, under Add a discount code, or at checkout. If you followed a link with a code in it, we've saved it for you and it comes off at checkout.

Delivery

How much is delivery?

UK delivery is £4.99, or free when your order is £75 or more.

How long will my order take?

Usually 5–10 working days from when you order: 2–5 days to make it, then the post. Each product page shows an estimated delivery date.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

Not yet. We only deliver to UK addresses for now.

How do I track my order?

We email you a tracking link when your order is posted. If your order has several pieces, they may come in separate parcels, each with its own tracking.

Returns and refunds

Can I return something?

Yes. Tell us within 14 days of delivery, then send it back within 14 days of telling us. Return postage is yours to pay unless the piece is faulty. We refund you within 14 days of getting it back, including the delivery charge you paid. How to return something.

Can I swap for a different size?

Because every piece is made to order, the quickest way is to return it for a refund and order the size you need.

What if my order arrives faulty or wrong?

Email pluge.shop@outlook.com within 30 days with your order number and a photo. We'll replace it or refund you, and you won't pay to send anything back.

Sizing and fit

How do your clothes fit?

Hoodies, crews and tees are a classic relaxed fit: take your usual size, or one size up for an oversized look. Joggers are relaxed and tapered, so take your usual size.

Where's the size guide?

On every product page, under Size & fit. There's also a size finder: measure a top you already like across the chest and it suggests your size.

What size is the model wearing?

Product pages show the model's height and size next to their photos, where we have them.

Can the site remember my size?

Yes. The first time you add a top, joggers or socks to your bag, we remember that size and pick it for you next time. You can change your sizes in your account.

Products and care

How do I wash my pieces?

Hoodies, crews, tees and joggers: wash at 30°C inside out with similar colours. No bleach. Tumble dry low or line dry. Don't iron over the print or dry clean.

Caps: spot clean only. Beanies: hand wash cold and dry flat. Socks: wash at 30°C inside out. Don't tumble dry.

Why does my tee look slightly different from the photo?

Our tees are garment-dyed, which makes them soft from the first wear, and each one takes the dye a little differently. Screens show colours differently too.

Will sold-out pieces come back?

Most Drop 01 pieces are made to order, so they rarely sell out. If a size does, choose it on the product page and leave your email, and we'll tell you if it comes back. Drops can close, though, so sign up for the drop alert to hear about new pieces first.

How do reviews work?

Sign in, open a product page and choose Write a review. We read every review before it goes up, and publish genuine ones whether they're good or bad. Reviews from people who've ordered that piece are marked "verified buyer".

Your account

How do I reset my password?

Open Account and choose Forgot your password? We'll email you a link to set a new one.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account and choose Delete account. Your saved pieces and bag go straight away. We keep order records for six years because tax law requires it. See Privacy and cookies.
